Quick flight guide

1) Flaps 10° (set). Release brakes, throttle to full, keep straight with the rudder.  2) At 55 kt ease back, climb at ~75 kt, raise the gear.  3) Trim for level flight; cruise ~100 kt.  4) Land: throttle back, flaps 20–30°, gear down, approach ~65 kt, flare gently, brake. Stall speed ≈ 48 kt (flaps up) – don't fall below it!
